Questions you may have before we talk

How long is the free consultation?

The call lasts 15 minutes. You can tell me what you're looking for, ask questions and get a sense of whether you want to schedule a therapy session.

Do I have to start therapy afterward?

No. The consultation is a conversation, not a commitment. You can decide afterward whether you want to begin.

Can I contact you about my teenager?

Yes. You can reach out about therapy for your teenager or about support for yourself as a parent.

Can I use insurance?

I accept private insurance. Please ask about your plan before booking a session so we can discuss your coverage and costs.

Can we meet online or speak Spanish?

I offer telehealth and sessions in Spanish or English. We can confirm whether online sessions are available for your location during the consultation.

What are your credentials?
