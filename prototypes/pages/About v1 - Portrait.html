About Gladys Henriquez

I work with parents, teens and adults who are trying to make sense of what keeps happening, what is hurting and what they want to be different.

I start by listening to how you understand your own life. What has been hard? What has helped before? What have you been carrying for a long time? I also pay attention to strengths you may not be giving yourself credit for.

Therapy should feel like a real conversation

You do not have to say the right thing in therapy. We'll talk honestly about what you want to change and pay attention to whether the work is actually helping. If something does not feel useful, tell me. I would rather adjust than keep doing something simply because it is what we started with.

Some sessions may include a creative activity or, with your agreement, a mindfulness walk. We decide together whether either one fits the work we are doing. You can always choose to keep our sessions focused on conversation.

I respect your pace. Change can be uncomfortable, and some parts take time. Being patient with yourself is part of the work.

I want to understand the life around the problem

What happens around you affects how you feel. What happens at home, at school or at work matters. So do your culture, relationships and experiences of change or loss.

My work with families, schools and community programs has taught me to pay attention to context. I want to understand what you're feeling and what has been happening around you.

My background

My background includes graduate mental health counseling training at NYU Steinhardt and work with children and families, teens and adults.

I'm a Registered Circle of Security Parenting Facilitator and the founder and Executive Director of Discover and Grow. I also continue developing my work in expressive arts-informed counseling.

I work under the practice of John Orr, LMHC, and offer sessions in English and Spanish.
