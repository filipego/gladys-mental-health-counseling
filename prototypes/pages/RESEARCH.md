# About-page research — Mobbin

The Word document was read before research. About must introduce Gladys, explain working together, describe context, provide factual background, and invite a consultation. No new professional claims or testimonials are needed.

## References visually inspected

1. [Headspace — Our Story](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/6ead3fea-5010-4a5c-9986-3ef86b9b9993): large portrait beside narrow story copy. Useful: person-first scale and legible measure. Not adopted: decorative circles or branding.
2. [Calm — Tamara Levitt biography](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/a9638d70-387d-4baf-b907-2e8ad6de683a): name, portrait and clear biography sequence. Useful: focused reading. Not adopted: circular portrait or centered long copy.
3. [Calm — Meet our instructor](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/901dd686-eafb-4e12-9350-4a3c1a139a5b): visual introduction followed by identity. Useful: clear connection between image and person; not copied as a video component.
4. [Headspace — Andy Puddicombe](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/d29e5a83-421e-411f-8316-ec305c44daac): concise biography without a credentials-card grid. Its centered text and patterned background are not suitable to inherit.
5. [KÖPPEN — Philosophy](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/540eb76e-09ed-4538-9caf-3201ee806bdb): asymmetric image/text scale. Useful for editorial rhythm, not its product imagery or palette.
6. [Hims — Care philosophy](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/296c6a7b-4ebc-40c9-ad0d-141c96d79ab1): care statement balanced with strong imagery. Avoid supplement associations and highlighted marketing words.
7. [Face Formula — About](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/c5b63eb3-7479-4690-a8f4-dc73f162a655): staggered text and image sequence. Useful: varied reading rhythm; not its skincare claims, intimate imagery or serif typography.

The searches returned adjacent healthcare/wellness and practitioner-profile references, not a verified set of private therapist practice websites. These are composition references, not evidence of clinical effectiveness or current design trends.

## Synthesis

Give a real portrait priority, keep narrative copy readable, explain the relationship before listing background, and let one invitation close the story. Distinguish the three demos structurally while retaining homepage v4's identity. Do not use alternating full-bleed stripes, numbered eyebrows, repetitive cards or invented testimonials.

Navigation: separate pages, as explicitly specified in the source. The Home prototype's existing anchors are an unfinished prototype convention, not the intended information architecture.
