## Structure and writing decisions

Four main pages: Home, About Gladys, Therapy & Support, Get Started. Privacy belongs in the footer as a utility page once the site's actual data handling is known.

Parents are the first audience addressed. Teens and adults have clear sections on the same therapy page. Anxiety and trauma belong within that content. FAQs belong on Get Started.

Use Gladys's first-person voice throughout. Section notes below describe content placement, not visual design. No text above the opening headline. Photos, typography, motion and composition will be considered with the forthcoming references.

The primary action is “Request a free consultation.” All instances lead to /get-started#consultation. This wording matches a request form. Use “Schedule” only if visitors will actually choose and reserve a time.

## About Gladys

### 1. Introduction

Placement: name as the page heading, followed by her title and introduction. Include a supplied portrait.

#### About Gladys Henriquez

Mental Health Counselor – Limited Permit (MHC-LP)

I work with parents, teens and adults. My approach begins with listening to how you understand your own life: what's difficult, what's helped before and what you hope will change.

I believe you bring strengths to that conversation, even when you're struggling to see them.

### 2. Working together

#### How we'll work together

We'll talk about what you want from therapy and pay attention to how the work is going. If something doesn't feel useful, I want you to tell me. We can adjust the approach as we learn more about what you need.

I respect your pace. Change can take time, and being patient with yourself is part of the work.

Some sessions may include a creative activity or, with your agreement, a mindfulness walk. These are options we discuss together. You can choose to keep our sessions focused on conversation.

#### Understanding your life outside therapy

What happens at home, at school or at work matters to our conversations. So do your culture, relationships and experiences of change or loss.

My work with families, schools and community programs has shaped how I understand people's difficulties. I want to learn about the circumstances around you as well as what you're feeling.

## Therapy & Support

### 1. Introduction

#### Therapy and parent support

I work with parents concerned about their children, teenagers dealing with pressures of their own, and adults seeking therapy. The focus of our sessions depends on what brings you here and what you want to work on.

Jump links: Parents → #parents | Teens → #teens | Adults → #adults

### 2. Parents

Anchor: #parents. Give this section the most space of the three audience sections.

#### Support for parents

It can be hard to know how to respond when your child is anxious, upset or pulling away. You might find yourself setting a boundary and then questioning it, or having the same argument without understanding why it keeps happening.

In our sessions, we can look closely at those moments. What happened before the conflict? What did each of you need? How could you respond next time?

We may work on communication and boundaries, understanding strong emotions, or repairing the connection after an argument. We can also make room for your own stress and the expectations you carry as a parent.

My work with parents draws on my counseling and school experience, along with my training as a Registered Circle of Security Parenting Facilitator.

If you're seeking therapy for your teenager, you can contact me to discuss that too.

Button: Request a free consultation

### 3. Teens

Anchor: #teens. Address the teenager directly here.

#### Therapy for teens

You might be feeling pressure at school, worrying about friendships or having a hard time at home. Sometimes you know what's bothering you. Sometimes you just know that things haven't felt right lately.

We can start wherever you are. You can talk about anxiety, anger, identity or the future, and we can work on ways to handle situations that feel difficult.

I'll listen to your perspective and work with you at a pace you can manage. If words are hard to find, we can discuss using a creative activity to explore what you're feeling.

For parents: You can request the first consultation about support for your teenager. We can discuss the next steps during that call.

Button: Request a free consultation

### 4. Adults

Anchor: #adults.

#### Therapy for adults

You may spend a lot of time going over conversations, anticipating problems or questioning your decisions. Perhaps you understand a pattern in your life but still find yourself repeating it.

We can examine what keeps that pattern going and work on different ways to respond. Our conversations may include difficult past experiences, family expectations, relationships or the demands of everyday life.

There is also room to work through grief and life changes, talk about boundaries, or understand reactions that feel stronger than you expected.

Button: Request a free consultation

### 5. Approaches

Placement: a plain explanation after the audience sections. Keep methods together rather than creating separate service pages. The specific approach list is inherited from the prior blueprint and needs source confirmation.

#### What our sessions may involve

Our work may draw on cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), acceptance and commitment therapy (ACT), psychodynamic or narrative approaches, mindfulness and trauma-informed care.

We might examine a recurring thought, talk about how an earlier experience affects you now, or practice a response to a difficult situation. I'll explain what we're trying and ask how it's working for you.

With your agreement, we may use expressive art or a mindfulness walk as part of a session. We can discuss these options if they seem useful to the work we're doing.

### 6. Session options

#### Meeting in person or online

I offer in-person sessions at 55 Broadway Ave, New York, NY, and telehealth sessions. We can discuss location requirements for telehealth when you contact me.

Sessions are available in English and Spanish. Fees start at $200, and I accept private insurance. Please ask about coverage for your plan before beginning therapy.

Button: Request a free consultation
