# Who I work with — visual bar

- The section is full-bleed paper with one quiet rule at its top and bottom; all content sits on the same 1200px alignment axis as the rest of the page.
- The heading row uses the prototype's `1fr / 1.05fr` split: a 44px heading leads on the left while the two-line practice note sits at the far right of the second column.
- Heading-to-content spacing is deliberately generous at roughly 76px; the section does not use cards, colored panels, badges, or decorative containers.
- Each audience row is a 148px editorial strip. The audience name anchors the left edge, a 200×148 image occupies the middle of the left column, and the description plus link begins on the right-column axis.
- The three rows are separated by approximately 96px of open space rather than borders, creating a slow, calm reading cadence.
- Audience descriptions use restrained 15px body copy with a maximum measure near 56ch; the action is a quiet underlined text link with a right arrow.
- Photography is landscape-oriented, lightly desaturated at rest, and becomes fully saturated on hover or keyboard focus; corners remain subtle rather than card-like.
- On narrow screens, preserve the hierarchy and content order—heading, note, audience name, image, description, link—while reflowing into one readable column instead of reproducing the prototype's overflow defect.
