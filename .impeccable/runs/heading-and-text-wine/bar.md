# Reference bar: wine heading-and-text sections

- The wine treatment is a full-bleed flat band (`#69434B` family), with no top or bottom rule competing with the color transition.
- Content stays on the existing 1200px page rail; the band changes the ground, not the site’s global bounds.
- The conversation variation is a full-width heading followed by a two-part editorial grid: a wider reading column and a narrower, larger statement column.
- The section heading keeps the shared H2 scale and a generous `clamp(40px, 5vw, 76px)` gap before the body.
- Standard paragraphs use the approved `on-wine` tint rather than pure white; the heading uses paper white.
- The statement is materially larger than body copy, constrained to about 24 characters, and uses the existing rose accent for hierarchy.
- Desktop spacing follows the About prototype’s chapter rhythm; mobile collapses to one column without introducing cards, borders, or decorative containers.
- Existing side-by-side content may opt into the same wine surface independently; its layout remains unchanged and its editorial link switches to the shared light treatment.
